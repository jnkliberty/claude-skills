# LinkedIn Algorithm Cheatsheet

**Source:** David Arnoux, viralbrain.ai — 62,130 viral posts, 3,150 creators (January 2026)

---

## BREW360 Algorithm

LinkedIn replaced 5+ legacy ranking systems with BREW360. This is the current algorithm.

### Old vs. New

| Old Rule | New Rule |
|---|---|
| First hour determined everything | Posts can resurface days later |
| Judged on early likes and velocity | AI reads meaning, intent, and relevance |
| Generic content could go viral | Niche-aligned content gets amplified |

### Profile as Classifier

BREW360 uses your profile as a classifier. It matches your content to readers based on profile-content alignment. If your profile says "GTM automation" but you post about cooking, the algorithm can't distribute it.

**Rule:** Post in your lane. Get amplified.

### Time of Day

**Midday window:** 12–2 PM UTC
**Afternoon window:** 3–5 PM UTC (equally strong)
**Best specific hours:** 12:00, 15:00, 22:00 UTC

Captures:
- European lunch/afternoon time
- US morning time
- Asian evening time

### Posting Frequency

3–5x per week. Not daily. Daily posting hurts reach.

**Frequency paradox:** Occasional posters show higher per-post engagement rates, but daily/active posters get more total reach over time. The sweet spot is 3–5x per week — enough volume for the algorithm to work with, enough spacing that each post gets room to breathe.

## Weighted Engagement Rate (WER)

**Formula:** (Likes + Comments x 5) / Followers x 100

Comments weighted 5x because they signal stronger intent and drive algorithmic momentum. A post with 10 comments is worth more to the algorithm than a post with 50 likes.

## Engagement Trend

Platform-wide engagement down 57% over 12 months (Aug 2025 peak to Jan 2026 low). Don't panic during slow periods — compare to your own baseline, not absolute numbers. The algorithm's distribution is tightening, which actually benefits niche-aligned small accounts.
